Interpreting those errors is more useful than reporting accuracy alone: missed churners represent overlooked retention opportunities, while false positives could consume outreach resources. Customer service calls, international-plan status, and daytime usage had the highest feature importances. I can explain their potential business relevance while recognizing that predictive associations do not establish causes. Together, the notebook's code, tree visualization, and written interpretations demonstrate my ability to connect preparation, modeling, and business communication.

## Where I am weak and what I still need to learn

My greatest uncertainty is determining which model and configuration are appropriate for a particular business problem. When several approaches seem plausible, I can find it difficult to distinguish a justified methodological choice from trial and error. The churn assignment illustrates this boundary in my preparation: I successfully applied a supplied workflow and suggested starting settings, but I still need practice developing and defending those choices independently. Although the notebook imports cross-validation and grid-search tools, it does not use them to compare configurations.

My goal is to approach experimentation more systematically: define the business objective, establish a baseline, compare a focused set of candidates through cross-validation, and reserve test data for final evaluation. I also need to strengthen my ability to assess regression assumptions, evaluate clusters without known labels, and validate time-series forecasts independently. In the areas covered by CIDM 5310, I need further practice processing unfamiliar image or audio data, diagnosing network data extraction failures, and developing reliable API and LLM workflows without relying heavily on established examples.

Considering how an analysis would operate in production reveals further gaps in my preparation. Performance on a classroom dataset offers limited evidence of how a model will respond to changes in customer behavior. I want a deeper understanding of data leakage, uncertainty, changing data distributions, and the relationship between evaluation metrics and business costs. In my professional context, this also means questioning whether regional patterns in payment-platform metadata reflect meaningful performance differences or variations in traffic volume and client mix. Developing the judgment to recognize these issues is an important part of becoming a more independent analyst.
